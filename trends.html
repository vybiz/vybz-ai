VYBZ Trend Radar

Source: YouTube music chart (official API). Nothing is scraped.

How the score works

Measured: views, likes, publish time. Computed: views per hour (growth velocity, 60%) and like rate (engagement, 40%), each ranked within this chart. Not available here: cross-platform presence, saturation, audience relevance, short-form fit, and history. TikTok and Instagram trend data needs their approved APIs. This is a snapshot of what is already popular, not a prediction that anything will go viral.
